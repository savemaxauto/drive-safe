In October we netted $6.14 per auto lead. At the midpoint unit cost, the kit nets about $9 before we sell a single lead. The lead pays us first, and every EPL dollar after that is margin with no CPL against it.

Kit contribution uses the cost sheet's landed range of about $9 to $23 per unit (published listings, not quotes). Refill cadence: Mintel consumer research found refills mostly replaced about every two months (dated 2014; a benchmark, not our data). Selling a lead requires consent language at checkout that counsel signs off.

Why a lead that stays is worth more04

Half of drivers shop every year. We'd be in the car when they do.

of auto insurance customers recently shopped their policy.

Record

switching in Q3 2026: more Americans switched personal-lines insurers than at any point on record.

6 mo

A form-fill lead is worth full price for about a week, then it's aged inventory. A driver who has our product on the vent and buys refills is someone we can reach, with permission, every time they're in market. That's a renewable lead, not a one-time sale.
