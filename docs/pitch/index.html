The idea03

The car freshener that saves lives.

It sits on the vent. It makes the car smell good. Every time you get in, one tap puts the phone away.

37%

The phone is the biggest problem in the car, and almost everyone has it. A product that lives in the car gives us a reason to stay with these drivers every day.

37%: AAA Foundation, 2023 Traffic Safety Culture Index (past 30 days); 85 million applies that to about 233 million licensed U.S. drivers (FHWA). 58% and 52%: Cambridge Mobile Telematics (2022 data). 315K and 3,208: NHTSA, 2024. +28%: Insurance.com. $98B: NHTSA economic cost estimate (2019 data). The tap starts a focus setting on the driver's own phone; it can't lock a phone, and its effect on driving is not yet tested.
