# Driver profile and Safe Miles (Nov 17, 2026)

How the app learns about a driver without a long form, and why we don't call it a driving score. The screens are in the mockups canvas: rows 2 and 4.

## Download to first drive
1. Welcome: what it is, how to use it, what you can earn.
2. Create account: first name and mobile number (text code), or Apple or Google. Nothing else.
3. Pair: hold the phone to the freshener.
4. What pauses: pick apps. Calls, maps, music and podcasts always stay on.
5. Allow Drive Mode: explain the iPhone Screen Time prompt before it appears (what it can and can't do).
6. Turn on Safe Miles: location, motion, phone handling and notifications, each its own switch, all off to start, with Turn on all.
7. Ready: a checklist, a first-drive bonus, and a pretend drive to try it.

## What we ask, and when
| When | We ask | Why it helps the driver | What it gives us |
|---|---|---|---|
| Download | First name, mobile number | Saves points, signs them in | A contact (no sales calls until they ask) |
| First drive | Year, make, model (or plate lookup) | Oil change and tire deals that fit the car | Vehicle |
| Day 3 | ZIP code | Car wash and gas deals nearby | Pricing area, which insurers can quote |
| Week 2 | Insurer and renewal month | A check-in a month before renewal | When they shop, current carrier |
| Month 1 | About what they pay a month (range) | Shows what Safe Miles could save | Premium band, savings estimate |
| At renewal, only if they compare | Birth date, address, other drivers, email | Quotes in one tap | A complete lead, consent per insurer |

Rules: one question at a time, every question has Skip, answers never earn points, and nothing goes to an insurer until the driver asks for quotes and picks who.

## Why "Safe Miles," not a score
- A score is a grade that can drop. Drivers read it as something that could raise their rate, and most insurer programs have taught them that (in Maryland only about 31% of enrolled policies went down).
- Safe Miles is a balance that only goes up. Every mile with the phone down, near the limit and with smooth stops, starts and turns counts. A rough drive earns fewer; it never takes any away.
- Four promises the design keeps: it only goes up; you hold it and see exactly what is shared; it goes only to insurers that use it for discounts; you can skip any question and delete anything.
- Insurers still get what they need: miles on record and the share that counted (for example 3,380 of 3,672, or 92%).

## To check
- Trademark search for "Safe Miles." USAA runs SafePilot Miles and OnMyWay pays "for every safe mile." Backup name: Good Miles.
- Carrier contracts must allow "discounts only." That's a new question for the carrier conversations: will they agree never to surcharge from Safe Miles?
- Offering Google sign-in on iPhone also means offering Sign in with Apple or a similar private option (App Store rules).
- Counsel on consent wording and on asking for birth date and address only at quote time.
