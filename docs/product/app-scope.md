# App scope for launch (Nov 17, 2026)

What the first version of the app needs, and what waits. Every screen listed is in the mockups canvas.

## Launch
| Area | Screens | Why it's essential |
|---|---|---|
| Setup | Welcome, create account, pair, what pauses, allow Drive Mode, turn on Good Miles, ready | Gets a driver from download to a first phone-free drive in about two minutes |
| Driving | Drive Mode, app paused, incoming call, drive summary | The core loop. End anytime, calls always ring |
| Good Miles | Good Miles with weekly goal, trips | Weekly goals and points are what cut phone use in the research (PNAS 2024). Trips let a driver mark a passenger ride so it never counts |
| Rewards | Rewards, scent and refill box | Rewards keep drivers coming back. The refill box is the second revenue line |
| Better rate | Savings, renewal check-in, pick who quotes, compare rates, six months later | Where the lead is created, with consent per insurer |
| Account | Profile, one question at a time, sharing, settings | A driver can see, change or delete everything. In-app account deletion is an App Store rule |
| Alerts | Live drive on the lock screen, weekly recap, refill reminder, renewal check-in | Nothing is sent while driving |

## How Drive Mode starts (Settings)
- Tap the freshener (always works).
- Tap, or when the phone joins the car's Bluetooth or CarPlay (default). Covers the days a driver forgets to tap.
- Remind me to tap when the phone joins the car.

## Sensor bonus
- 25 points for each Good Miles sensor (location during drives, motion, phone handling, notifications), plus 100 for turning on all four: 200 in all.
- Drivers keep the points if they turn a sensor off later, so the bonus never feels like a trap.
- In California, a reward for sharing data is a financial incentive and needs a short notice explaining it. The "How it works" link on the opt-in screen holds it.
- Cost: 200 points is about an eighth of a free refill, roughly 50 cents a driver.
- Points still never come from asking for quotes or answering profile questions.

## Later
- Gift mode: a parent sends a freshener to a new driver, and the first tap shows their note.
- Invite a friend for points, to lower the cost of winning each customer.
- Family accounts for parents and new drivers. Needs counsel on minors' data.
- Friend leaderboards. The research used one, but it's social and can wait.
- CarPlay and Android Auto screens. CarPlay needs Apple's approval for the app category.
