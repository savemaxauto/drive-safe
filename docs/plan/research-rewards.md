# Rewards research notes (Nov 17, 2026)

Behind the deck's rewards and two-path slides. Every figure came from search summaries; none of the source pages could be opened from this session, so verify before external use.

## What changes phone use
- Points, weekly goals and a leaderboard with no cash cut handheld phone use 20.5%; adding a $2,000 shared cash pool raised it to 27.6%. Effects held up to 65 days after. Ebert/Delgado et al., PNAS 2024, 1,653 Progressive Snapshot drivers. https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2320603121
- Cash-only needs about $7/week ($50 over 7 weeks), paid weekly and loss-framed, with feedback: −21%. Doubling to $14/week added nothing. Lump sums and incentive-only arms did nothing. Effects waned after payments stopped. Delgado et al., JAMA Netw Open 2024. https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2820970
- Phone mounts and commitment contracts: no effect (same PNAS trial).

## Precedents
- Nationwide Focused Driving Rewards: e-gift cards for phone-free driving, no policy needed; up to $60 per 6 months under 30, $30 over 30. https://news.nationwide.com/distraction-and-anxiety-accelerate-issues-on-roadways/
- Safe 2 Save (Texas): points redeemed at 1,500+ merchant locations, merchant-funded, ~230k users (self-reported). https://safe2save.org/
- This App Saves Lives (shut down 2024) and Miles (shut down May 2025): merchant-funded points built audiences but no revenue.
- Allstate Rewards points: ended Oct 2023. Plymouth Rock Road Rewards: active.
- Jerry DriveShield: rewards from 50+ partners. https://getjerry.com/driveshield
- Boston's Safest Driver contest: $25,950 prize pool funded by Liberty Mutual.

## Who pays
- Upside: merchants fund gas cash back (~11¢/gal) from incremental profit; affiliate pays ~$10 per referred user's first purchase. https://upside.com/data-and-security/grocery-contract-methodology
- Car washes: Mister Car Wash ~2.5M members, ~$29.56/mo revenue per member; member LTV ~$440 (Rinsed). A $20–40 bounty per new member is our estimate.
- Affiliates: Tire Rack 6%, Discount Tire 4%, Advance Auto 4%, Valvoline 3–7%, Amazon automotive ~4.5%.
- Gift-card APIs (Tremendous, Tango Card, Giftbit): no platform fee; face value; 0% if funded by ACH.
- Estimated split: ~$40/yr perceived rewards per driver, ~70% partner-funded, ~$11–12 self-funded.

## Retail costs
- Gas $3.87/gal (AAA, Nov 13, 2026) → ~$58 a 15-gallon fill. Car wash ~$24 single / ~$34 a month unlimited. Oil change ~$35 conventional. Tire rotation $20–50. New tire ~$212 + $31 install.

## Design rules used in the plan
- Play first (points, streaks, weekly goals) — costs nothing.
- Everyday rewards are cheap: our refills at cost, partner-funded coupons and cash back.
- Big rewards (full tank, tires, a year of car washes) only as monthly prize draws with a fixed pool.
- Rewards that cost us money go only to drivers who share their score.
- Weekly cadence, per-driver caps, no cash value, expire after inactivity; fund gift cards by ACH.
- Counsel: sweepstakes rules, insurance anti-rebating, taxes on gift-card rewards.
