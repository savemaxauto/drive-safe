THE REWARDS

Most of the rewards cost us little or nothing.

PLAY, EVERY DRIVE

Points, streaks and weekly goals

In a 2024 trial of 1,653 drivers, this alone cut phone use 20.5%. No cash was involved.

Costs us nothing

EARN, EVERY MONTH

Refills, car washes, oil changes, gas

We give free refills. Shops and gas stations pay for their own coupons and cash back.

Partners pay about 70%

WIN, EVERY MONTH

Draws for a full tank, tires or a year of car washes

Big prizes go out as drawings, the same way we run giveaways today.

Fixed budget, $6K to $60K a year

A driver gets about $40 a year in rewards. We pay about $12 of that, and only for drivers who share their score.

Points come from phone-free driving, never from asking for quotes. PNAS 2024; Upside; Nationwide; Mister Car Wash. Estimates.

The research on what changes behavior is pretty clear. Points, streaks and weekly goals cut phone use by about a fifth with no money involved, and the effect lasted after the program ended. Getting the same result with cash takes about $7 a week, which a lead business can’t afford. So the everyday rewards are cheap. We give free refills, and the car wash, oil change and gas offers are paid for by the shops and stations because they want new customers. The big prizes, like a full tank or a set of tires, go out as monthly drawings. We keep each month’s prizes under $5,000 so we don’t need the New York and Florida bonds. One rule we never break: points come from phone-free driving, never from asking for quotes. Several lead buyers ban leads from people who were rewarded for signing up, the FTC fined Fluent $2.5 million over that model, and some states treat even a $5 gift card for a quote as an illegal inducement. Counsel reviews the draw rules and the rebating rules before launch.
