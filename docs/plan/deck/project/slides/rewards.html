THE REWARDS

Most of the rewards cost us little or nothing.

PLAY, EVERY DRIVE

Points, streaks and weekly goals

In a 2024 trial of 1,653 drivers, this alone cut phone use 20.5%. No cash was involved.

Costs us nothing

EARN, EVERY MONTH

Car washes, oil changes, refills and gas cards

Partners pay for their own prizes. Refills cost us $1.30. Gas cards take the most points.

No prize costs us over $2 per 1,000 points

WIN, EVERY MONTH

Draws for a full tank or $500 toward tires

Big prizes go out as drawings, the same way we run giveaways today.

Fixed budget, $6K to $60K a year

1,000 points is worth about $12 to a driver and costs us about 72 cents. A driver gets about $46 a year in prizes. We pay about $3.

Points never come from quotes. Worst case, every point spent on gas cards: $9.53 a driver a year.

The research on what changes behavior is pretty clear. Points, streaks and weekly goals cut phone use by about a fifth with no money involved, and the effect lasted after the program ended. Getting the same result with cash takes about $7 a week, which a lead business can’t afford. So the everyday rewards work like an arcade prize counter with grown-up rules. Every prize shows what it’s worth. Car wash, oil change and auto parts partners pay for their own prizes because they want new customers. Refills cost us $1.30 and are worth $8 to the driver. Gas cards cost us face value, so they take the most points, and no prize can cost us more than $2 per 1,000 points. That puts our cost at about $3 per driver a year, and about $9.50 even if every point went to gas cards, under the $12 we first budgeted. The big prizes, like a full tank or a set of tires, go out as monthly drawings. We keep each month’s prizes under $5,000 so we don’t need the New York and Florida bonds. One rule we never break: points come from phone-free driving, never from asking for quotes. Several lead buyers ban leads from people who were rewarded for signing up, the FTC fined Fluent $2.5 million over that model, and some states treat even a $5 gift card for a quote as an illegal inducement. Counsel reviews the draw rules and the rebating rules before launch.
