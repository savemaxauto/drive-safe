PNAS 2024 (Ebert et al.); Upside; Nationwide Focused Driving Rewards; Mister Car Wash. Rewards per driver are estimates.

The research is clear on what works. Points, streaks and a weekly goal cut phone use by about a fifth with no money at all, and the effect lasted after the program ended. Paying cash for the same result takes about seven dollars a week, which no lead business can afford. So everyday rewards are cheap: free refills that cost us little, plus car wash, oil change and gas offers that the shops and stations pay for because they want new customers. The big rewards, a full tank or a set of tires, are monthly drawings with a fixed prize pool, which is exactly what we already know how to run. Apps funded only by merchant points have shut down; ours survives because drivers buy the kit and refills. Counsel needs to review the sweepstakes rules and insurance anti-rebating rules, and rewards may be taxable.
