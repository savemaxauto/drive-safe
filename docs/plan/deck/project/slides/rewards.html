THE REWARDS

Most of the rewards cost us little or nothing.

PLAY, EVERY DRIVE

Points, streaks and weekly goals

In a 2024 trial of 1,653 drivers, this alone cut phone use 20.5%. No cash was involved.

Costs us nothing

EARN, EVERY MONTH

Refills, car washes, oil changes, gas

We give free refills. Shops and gas stations pay for their own coupons and cash back.

Partners pay about 70%

WIN, EVERY MONTH

Draws for a full tank, tires or a year of car washes

Big prizes go out as drawings, the same way we run giveaways today.

Fixed budget, $6K to $60K a year

A driver gets about $40 a year in rewards. We pay about $12 of that, and only for drivers who share their score.

PNAS 2024 (Ebert et al.); Upside; Nationwide Focused Driving Rewards; Mister Car Wash. Rewards per driver are estimates.

The research on what changes behavior is pretty clear. Points, streaks and weekly goals cut phone use by about a fifth with no money involved, and the effect lasted after the program ended. Getting the same result with cash takes about $7 a week, which a lead business can't afford. So the everyday rewards are cheap. We give free refills, and the car wash, oil change and gas offers are paid for by the shops and stations because they want new customers. The big prizes, like a full tank or a set of tires, go out as monthly drawings with a fixed budget, which we already know how to run. Apps that were funded only by merchant points have shut down. Ours has revenue from kits and refills. Counsel needs to look at the sweepstakes rules and the insurance anti-rebating rules, and gift-card rewards can be taxable.
