The freshener carries the business from day one. The lead streams compound on top, at almost no extra cost.

Be straight about the mix. In the base case the kit and refills are about 90% of 2029 revenue. Lead revenue is smaller because only opted-in buyers become leads, and only about half shop in a year. That’s fine: those leads cost us nothing extra to acquire, and they’re the part that grows as drivers stay. Carrier programs are real precedent (Cellcontrol, Root partnerships) but we don’t count a dollar until a carrier signs.
