The one-time cost to design our own housing and cut the molds is about $65 to $115 thousand, including counsel; the app is separate and comes later. Two numbers still need real quotes: the fragrance oil and the prototype run. The smart version, with a battery and Bluetooth, adds a few dollars a unit plus FCC testing, which is why version one stays mechanical.
