This is what the driver sees every day. Home is built like a ride app: a map and one bar that says tap your freshener. Tapping the freshener starts Drive Mode. The apps they picked are paused, calls, maps and music stay on, and they can end the drive anytime. Good Miles count up while they drive. After the drive they see what they earned, and the map hides where the trip started and ended. Points go to a prize counter, like an arcade, where every prize shows what it’s worth and partners pay for most of them. These are design mockups, not a built app.
