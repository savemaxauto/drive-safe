Lead with the trial: Penn researchers ran a randomized trial on about 2,000 Progressive Snapshot customers. Handheld phone use fell 15 to 21 percent, but only when feedback came with a financial reward. Feedback alone did nothing. That shapes our product: the app should reward phone-free streaks with refill credits. The demand side is clear: almost everyone knows it’s dangerous, most young adults want help disconnecting, and half would share a score for a better price.
