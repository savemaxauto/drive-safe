Russ will ask whether carriers really pay more for safe drivers, so here’s what we found. Carriers clearly pay more for drivers they expect to be safer. Allstate’s data company Arity built a lead platform so carriers can bid on a driver’s expected lifetime value, and it reports up to 25 percent more binds when carriers use driving data. Nobody has published what a carrier pays for a lead that carries an outside driving score, and EverQuote’s EverDrive app found one carrier partner and never reported results. So we assume 1.4 times a normal lead, about $37, which is the bind lift times the lifetime value lift. If Good Miles reach carriers through a filed scoring partner like CMT, so they can use them in pricing, it could be closer to two times. We only share phone-down miles, not speed or braking, which is a narrower signal. The important part is that the plan doesn’t depend on it.
