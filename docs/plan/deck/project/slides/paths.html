This is the real decision, and it doesn’t have to be made today. Both paths start with the same 90-day test. Path A keeps it small: a stock freshener with an NFC sticker, sold only on our own quote pages. It makes money every year and stays profitable even if nobody opts in to quotes. Path B is the bigger investment: our own press-to-puff design, the app, the rewards program and paid growth. It’s about six times the revenue and a much bigger 2029, but it puts about $400 thousand at risk and only works if the test numbers hold. The test tells us which one to take.
