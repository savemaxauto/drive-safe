Month-by-month model: docs/plan/cash.py. Path B includes the team, app, rewards and prize draws.

Both paths start with the same test, so we don’t have to pick one today. Path A stays small. We sell a stock freshener with an NFC sticker on our own quote pages, the most we ever have out is about $36 thousand, and we’re back to even by October 2027. Path B is the bigger version, with our own design, the app, the rewards and paid growth. It’s about six times the revenue, but it puts about $450 thousand out at the low point in the summer of 2028 and pays back in October 2029. The test tells us which one to take.
