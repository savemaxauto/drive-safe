Russ will ask what the app really costs, so here it is from the bottom up. Launching on iPhone, with the app, the backend and an admin console, is about 3,650 hours. At a nearshore agency that’s about $274 thousand and five months. Android and the growth features, like boosts and the prize draw, are another $109 thousand the next year. A US agency would cost about $890 thousand for the same work. Running it is about $99 thousand a year once our own engineer takes over maintenance, including hosting, security testing and the SOC 2 report insurers will ask for. The plan used to carry $150 thousand. The full cost is now in every number in this deck. With rewards costing less than we first assumed, Path B pays back about a month later than before. The admin console is how the team runs it: drivers, points, prizes, partners and rules, with every change logged.
