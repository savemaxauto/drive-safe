Concept drawing, not to scale. The 90-day test uses a stock freshener; this design comes with our own housing.

This is what we have in mind. Like Drift, the face is a material you’d want in your car: stone, wood or metal, and it swaps. Behind it is an NFC tag and a small puff pump. When you get in, you press your phone to the face. That one motion turns on Drive Mode and pushes a burst of scent through the refill, like taking a breath of fresh air. It’s the reward for putting the phone away. Version one is purely mechanical: no battery, no charging. A later smart version could release the scent only once the phone is actually locked.
