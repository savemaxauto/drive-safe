The closest match is Arity. Allstate’s data arm pays apps like GasBuddy and Life360 to embed its SDK, then sells driving-scored audiences and leads through its own lead businesses. That proves carriers will pay for scored drivers. It also shows the trap: Texas is suing because consent was buried. Drift proves people will pay $12 to $16 a refill, on subscription, for a car scent that looks good: it reached about $6M in recurring revenue and sold to Scentbird in 2022. FIXD proves a cheap car gadget can become a subscription, and it never added insurance leads. That gap is ours.
