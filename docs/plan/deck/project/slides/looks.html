DESIGN OPTIONS

Three looks we could make. Wood is the place to start.

WOOD SLAB

Closest to Drift’s look

Thin wood face. The phone reads the tag through it. Light on the vent.

Mid cost. Our pick to start.

STONE PEBBLE

Most premium feel

Stone or ceramic face. Heaviest, so it needs a stronger clip. NFC needs a prototype check.

Highest cost

SOFT FABRIC

Most colors, lowest cost

Knit fabric face. Lightest, and the tag reads easily through it.

Lowest cost

During the test we show photos of all three on the thank-you page and see which one people pick.

There are three directions we could take the look. The wood slab is closest to what Drift proved people buy, it's light enough for a vent, and the phone reads the tag through thin wood. Stone feels the most premium, but it's heavy and we'd need to check the tag reads through it. Fabric is the cheapest and comes in any color, so it's a good second line. We don't have to guess. During the test we can show photos of all three on the thank-you page and count which one people pick.
