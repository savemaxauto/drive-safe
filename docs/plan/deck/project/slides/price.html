A month of refills runs $5 at Bath & Body Works, $12 at Drift and $15 at Pura. Ours would be $8.

Retail prices, November 2026: Walmart, Target, Grove, Bloomingdale’s, Liberty London. Febreze and Little Trees hold about 65% of the $586M US market (Circana, 2023).

Price is one of the two numbers that decide Path B, so here’s where $29 sits. A Febreze clip is about $4. Drift’s starter kit is $13, but its refills run about $12 a month. Pura Car is $35 and Jo Malone is $70. We’d be priced like Pura, below the designer brands, with a reason to keep it that none of them have. Our refills at $8 a month would undercut Drift and Pura. To be fair, most of this market is cheap clips. Febreze and Little Trees sell about two thirds of it, so premium is a small slice. That’s why the test shows $29 against $24 instead of assuming it.
