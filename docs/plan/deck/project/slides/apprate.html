The lead we sell

This is where the money comes from. Good Miles are miles driven with the phone down. They only go up, so they never feel like a grade, and we never track speed or braking. The savings screen shows what a safe driver could save over one to three years, about $276 a year after a year on a $2,300 policy. A month before renewal the app checks in. The driver picks which insurers can quote them, with consent for each one, and that’s the lead we sell. Good Miles only go to insurers that use them for discounts, and comparing never earns points. Insurer names and prices are samples.
