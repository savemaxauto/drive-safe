THE ONE IDEA

Drivers pay us to become leads. Then they become the safest leads on the market.

Every lead we buy today costs $20. This one starts by earning $13.

This is the whole plan in two sentences. Today we pay about $20 for every lead. A $29 freshener earns about $13.61 after product, fulfillment and shipping before we sell a single lead. Over time, the drivers who use the app become scored, opted-in, safe drivers: the leads carriers want most.
