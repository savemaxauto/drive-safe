THE IDEA

Give drivers a reason to stay with us.

1

Buy

A driver buys our $29 car freshener for the vent.

2

Tap

Every drive, one tap on the freshener puts the phone away.

3

Earn

Phone-free streaks earn points for refills, car washes and gas.

4

Shop

At renewal they shop through us, as the safest, most valuable lead we sell.

The driver pays us up front, and we can sell their quote request each time they shop.

Walk through it the way the driver would. They buy a freshener because they want the car to smell good. Every time they get in, they tap their phone on it and the phone goes quiet. The app turns phone-free streaks into points for refills, car washes and gas, and if they agree, it builds a safe-driving score. When their policy comes up for renewal, they shop through us and we sell the quote request. Today we pay about $20 for a lead and sell it once. Here the driver pays us first, and we can sell their quote request each time they shop.
