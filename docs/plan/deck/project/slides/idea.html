THE IDEA, AS THE DRIVER LIVES IT

Give drivers a reason to stay with us.

1

Buy

A driver buys our $29 car freshener for the vent.

2

Tap

Every drive, one tap on the freshener puts the phone away.

3

Earn

Phone-free streaks earn points for refills, car washes and gas.

4

Shop

At renewal they shop through us, as the safest, most valuable lead we sell.

They pay us to start. We earn again every time they shop.

Walk it in the order the driver lives it. They buy a freshener because they want their car to smell good. Every time they get in, they tap their phone on it and the phone goes quiet. The app turns phone-free streaks into points they spend on refills, car washes and gas, and with their permission builds a safe-driving score. When their policy renews, they shop through us, and we sell them as a premium lead. Instead of paying $20 for a lead we sell once, the driver pays us first and we earn again every renewal.
