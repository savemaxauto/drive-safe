OUR PROMISE TO DRIVERS

A driver’s data only ever helps them.

Never used against you

We share a driving score only when it helps the driver. Never to raise a rate.

Sharing is a separate choice

The freshener, Drive Mode and partner offers work without sharing anything.

Safer drives pay off

Phone-free streaks earn points for refills, car washes and gas.

Insurers can’t make this promise. We can, because we aren’t the insurer.

This is what makes drivers say yes to us when they say no to insurers. We reach them before any policy exists, and we’re not the one setting their rate. So we can promise in writing: sharing is optional, safe driving earns rewards, and a driver’s score is only shared when it would help them, the way Jerry shares a score only when it lowers a premium. A driver who doesn’t share still gets the freshener, Drive Mode and partner offers. Counsel needs to approve the exact wording of “never used against you” before any driver sees it.
