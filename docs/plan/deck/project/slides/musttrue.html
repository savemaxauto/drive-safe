This is what could go wrong in Path B, one change at a time. Most of the risks only push the payback date back a few months. Two of them double the cash we need: if the kit only sells at $24, or if paid ads cost five dollars more per customer than planned. Both push the most cash we’d have out to about a million dollars and the payback to the end of 2030. The test checks the price directly by showing $29 and $24 on the thank-you page.
