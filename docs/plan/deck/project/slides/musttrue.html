Here’s what could go wrong in Path B, one change at a time. Most of these only push the payback date back by a few months. Two of them matter much more. If the kit only sells at $24, or if paid ads cost five dollars more per customer than planned, the most cash we’d have out rises to about $700 thousand and payback slips to the middle of 2030. The test checks price directly by showing $29 and $24 on the thank-you page. Paid ads we try with a small budget before we commit to scaling them.
