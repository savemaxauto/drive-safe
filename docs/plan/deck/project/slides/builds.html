THREE WAYS TO BUILD IT

The scent burst pays for itself at about $35.

1. BASIC

Scent pad, NFC tag, no moving parts

$1.55 to $5.30 a unit
$25K to $45K to develop
In production in 4 to 8 months
Works at $29

Low difficulty

2. PRESS-TO-PUFF

Press pushes a puff through the refill

$1.90 to $8.30 a unit
$40K to $115K to develop
In production in 6 to 11 months
Needs about $35

Medium. Our pick if the puff test passes.

3. POWERED BURST

Battery and fan fire a burst

$5.50 to $9.65 a unit
$60K to $150K to develop
In production in 7 to 12 months
Needs $35 or more

High. Batteries hate hot cars.

During the test we show the burst version at $35 next to the basic one at $29, before we build either.

There are three ways to build this. The basic version has no moving parts. It’s the cheapest and fastest, and it works at $29, but the only reward is the tap itself. The press-to-puff version adds a small pump or bellows so pressing your phone on the face pushes out a burst of scent, with no battery. It costs a little more per unit and takes a few more months, and it only pays for itself if we can charge about $35, which is where Pura Car sits. At $35 it’s actually the best version on the numbers. The powered version uses a battery and a fan. It gives the strongest burst, but lithium batteries are rated to about 60 degrees and dashboards reach 70 to 90 degrees in summer, so it’s the hardest to make safe. We don’t have to choose now. During the test we show the burst version at $35 next to the basic one at $29 and see which people pick.
