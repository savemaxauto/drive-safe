Insurers want to see how people drive, especially phone use. Nationwide now pays up to $60 in gift cards every six months for phone-free driving, even to people who aren’t its customers. Root gives its safest drivers up to 44 percent off, scored partly on phone use. But drivers keep saying no: only about one in eight is enrolled in an insurer program, and about 70 percent worry about the privacy of that data. Buying the data secondhand now gets you sued. The insurer is the one party drivers don’t want watching.
