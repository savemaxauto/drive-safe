Carriers want driving data because driving behavior predicts claims: Progressive found its riskiest drivers cost about 2.5 times its safest. But most drivers won’t give it to an insurer, and buying it secondhand now carries legal risk. Clean, consented driving data is getting scarcer, which is exactly why owning it is valuable.
