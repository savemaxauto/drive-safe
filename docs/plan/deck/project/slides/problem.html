Insurers know driving behavior predicts claims: holding a phone raises crash risk about 3.6 times in a peer-reviewed study of real drivers. That’s why money is pouring in: Cambridge Mobile Telematics raised $350 million this year with State Farm and Allianz behind it. But drivers keep saying no. Only about one in eight is enrolled, and about 70 percent worry about the privacy of that data. Buying the data secondhand now gets you sued. The insurer is the one party drivers don’t want watching.
