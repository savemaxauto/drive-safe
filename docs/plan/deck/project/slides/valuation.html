This is the strategic case. Lead-gen revenue is valued at well under one times revenue because it depends on carrier ad budgets that can vanish overnight: in 2023 EverQuote lost almost half its quarterly revenue when carriers pulled back. Revenue that comes from drivers, like kits and refills, doesn’t move with carrier budgets and is valued several times higher. Building this line makes Save Max both more valuable and more stable.
