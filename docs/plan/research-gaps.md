# Open-gap research (Nov 17, 2026)

All figures came from search summaries. The source pages could not be opened from this session, so check them before the deck goes outside the company.

## Price
- Febreze vent clip about $3 to $4. Yankee Smart Scent clip $4.98.
- Drift wood starter kit $12.95 to $12.99. Refill $11.99, or about $11 a month on subscription.
- Bath & Body Works car holder $12.95, refills $4.95 each (3 for $11).
- Pura Car $34.99, Car Pro $65.99, refills $14.99 to $15.99 for about 4 weeks.
- Jo Malone car diffuser $70, refill $45. Diptyque car diffuser about $120.
- US auto air freshener sales $586M (Circana via Happi, 2023). Febreze and Little Trees about 65% of it.
- Takeaway: $29 sits in the gap between mass clips and designer diffusers. An $8 refill undercuts Drift and Pura. Premium is a small slice of the market, so test $29 against $24.

## Refill cost
- Fragrance oil about $15 to $55 per kg. At 2 to 6 mL a refill, the oil is about $0.03 to $0.33.
- Empty 5 to 8 mL diffuser bottle $0.19 to $0.28 at 2,000+.
- Finished private-label refill diffuser $1.85 to $2.15 at 100 to 3,000 units.
- Custom fragrance development $1,500 to $12,000 plus a 15 to 25 lb oil minimum. Use a stock scent for the test.
- The model uses $1.30 a refill ($3.90 for a box of 3) to stay on the safe side.

## Prototypes and lead time
- 3D print $20 to $200+ a part, CNC $100 to $1,000+, urethane casting $200 to $5,000+.
- China samples 5 to 10 days, production 15 to 30 days, air 5 to 10 days door to door. Ocean adds 3 to 6 weeks.
- US promotional suppliers ship logo vent fresheners in 7 to 15 business days, MOQ 100 to 150, about $3 each at 1,000. This is the route for the 90-day test.

## NFC
- NTAG213 works through plastic, wood, glass and fabric. Stone weakens the signal and fabric can detune it. Metal needs an on-metal tag.
- Tiny tags fail at 2 to 5 mm. Use a 20 to 30 mm tag.
- iPhone XS and later: an NFC personal automation can run immediately (iOS 17) and set Driving Focus. Each user builds it once in Shortcuts. Android has no built-in NFC trigger and needs an app.

## Prize draws
- New York and Florida require registration and a bond when total prizes in one promotion are over $5,000. Keep each monthly draw under that.
- A free way to enter is required.
- Administration runs about $600 to $3,000+ a promotion. Use one set of rules for repeated draws.

## Anti-rebating
- NAIC model (2020) allows value-added services and free raffles. No national dollar cap.
- New York has treated a $5 gift card for requesting a quote as an illegal inducement. Washington caps gifts at $100 a year. North Dakota bars gifts conditioned on a quote.
- Rule for the plan: rewards come from phone-free driving and partners, never from asking for quotes.

## Consent and lead buyers
- The FCC one-to-one consent rule was vacated in January 2025. Written consent has no federal expiry, but aged leads sell for far less and consent certificates expire after 90 days unless retained.
- Plan a fresh quote request in the app at renewal, which creates new consent.
- Fluent, IntegrityCONNECT and several insurance affiliate offers ban leads from people rewarded with points, entries or prizes for signing up. The FTC fined Fluent $2.5M in 2023 over that model.
- Ask our buyers and any marketplaces how they treat traffic from a product with a rewards app before the test.
