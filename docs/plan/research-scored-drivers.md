# Do carriers pay more for scored drivers? (Nov 17, 2026)

All figures came from search summaries; check the sources before external use. "Vendor" marks a company's own claim.

## Short answer
Partly. Carriers clearly pay more for drivers they expect to be safer, and pay extra for data to find them. Nobody has published a per-lead premium for a lead that carries an outside driving score.

## Evidence for
- Arity (Allstate) bought Transparent.ly and LeadCloud and launched Driving Risk Audiences (about 100M drivers) so marketers can bid on expected lifetime value. Arity says the lowest-risk tier is worth up to 5x the average driver's lifetime value (vendor). https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/now-insurance-marketers-can-optimize-their-lead-gen-investments-to-maximize-life-time-value-for-their-company-301409366.html
- Arity IQ at time of quote: double-digit bind-rate gains, near 25% in some channels; 10% conversion lift in preferred segments; modeled 16% lifetime value gain (vendor).
- Arity launched a carrier-controlled Lead Platform in Sep to Oct 2026. Arity revenue rose in Q2 and Q3 2026 on "higher lead generation advertising sales."
- Arity and Experian (2026): shoppers can see an Arity Driving Report and choose to share driving data before a policy is bound. This is the closest competitor to our "before the insurer" position.
- MediaAlpha filings: carriers bid on expected lifetime value across 35+ attributes.
- Progressive: riskiest drivers' loss costs about 2.5x the safest. LexisNexis: forecasted loss ratio about 135% at the lowest scores vs 38% at the highest (vendor).

## Evidence against
- EverDrive (EverQuote, 2016): 500k downloads by 2017, safe-driver offers through Octo and State Auto in 2018 (discounts up to 50%, not a higher lead price). No results disclosed. App last updated Dec 2019. State Auto was absorbed by Liberty Mutual in 2022.
- Raw driving data sells for cents (Verisk paid automakers 26 to 61 cents a car).
- Arity's lead revenue swings and the unit is losing money.
- A score we build is not filed with regulators, so carriers can bid with it but not price with it. Using a score in pricing can bring FCRA duties (Life360's Arity program sends adverse action notices).

## Price assumption
| Case | Multiple | Price | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Floor | 1.0x | $26.47 | Carriers ignore the score |
| Plan | 1.4x | about $37 | Bind rate +25% times lifetime value +16% (Arity IQ, vendor) |
| Possible | about 2.1x | about $56 | Fair share of value if the score is usable in pricing (filed score partner such as CMT) |
| Ceiling | about 3.9x | about $102 | Carrier break-even (our estimate) |

Value math (estimate): average premium $2,240; a safe driver with 25% lower loss cost and a 10% discount adds about $224 a year of profit, about $896 over four years. With bind rate rising from about 5.3% to 6.6% per lead, a safe-driver lead is worth about $76 more to the carrier.
