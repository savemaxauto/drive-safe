THE PRODUCT

One product, three layers.

2027

1. The freshener

$29 vent clip with an NFC tap point and a refill club. No battery, no tracking. It’s the habit that keeps us in the car.

2028

2. The app

Free. One tap starts Drive Mode. With opt-in, it builds a Safe Drive Score from phone use, speed and braking.

2028–29

3. Safe-driver leads

At renewal, opted-in drivers become leads, priced higher when their score shows they drive safely.

Each layer pays for itself before the next one is built.

Layer one is a consumer product people buy for the smell and the cue. Layer two is a free app, like Brick’s, that turns the tap into a habit and, with permission, a driving score. Layer three is what we already do: sell auto insurance leads, now timed to renewal and priced on how they drive. We only fund the next layer when the last one hits its numbers.
