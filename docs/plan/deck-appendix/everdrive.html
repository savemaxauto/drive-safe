THE MOAT · A LESSON FROM EVERQUOTE

EverQuote tried a version of this. Here’s what’s different.

EVERDRIVE, 2016–2018

  • A free safe-driving app, built with CMT
  • 500k users, 781M miles logged in 2017
  • Added safe-driver insurance offers in 2018
  • Then went quiet. EverQuote also closed its own agency in 2023–25.

SAVE MAX LIVES

  • Starts with a product people pay for, so it funds itself
  • A cue on the vent and a monthly refill keep drivers engaged
  • Rewards phone-free streaks, as the trial says we must
  • Consent and lead sales designed in from day one

Free apps fade. Products people pay for, and refill, stay.

Be upfront: the closest attempt came from a lead-gen company like us. EverQuote’s EverDrive app reached half a million users and started offering safe-driver insurance quotes in 2018, then there’s no public record of it after that. We don’t know exactly why it stopped. Our reading: a free app has no revenue of its own and nothing physical to bring people back. Ours is paid for at the start and refilled every month, so it doesn’t depend on lead revenue to survive.
