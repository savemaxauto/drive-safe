FTC GM/OnStar order (Jan 2026); Texas v. Allstate/Arity (filed Jan 2025); Maryland MODPA (Apr 2026); FCC consent-revocation rules (Apr 2025).

The legal direction is clear: express, separate consent for driving and location data, and no selling raw location. Some states, like Maryland, ban the sale of precise location outright, so we sell a derived score or a lead flag only. If carriers used our score to set rates we could be treated as a consumer reporting agency, so at first the score only prices the lead. Counsel signs off on every consent screen before launch.
