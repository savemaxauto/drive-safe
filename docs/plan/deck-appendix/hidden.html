Teens are the biggest: adding a teen driver raises a family’s premium by about $3,500 a year, so those households shop hard, and parents already pay for monitoring (Life360 has 2 million US paying circles). Gig drivers need a rideshare endorsement most don’t have. Employers face nuclear verdicts for distracted employees. And every opted-in driver can also be a home, life or Medicare lead. None of this is in the forecast.
