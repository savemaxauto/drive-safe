NO BLUETOOTH CHIP

The phone already knows when it’s driving. Skipping BLE saves battery, FCC testing ($5–12K) and unit cost.

BUY THE SCORING

Start on Damoov’s SDK ($0–299 a month). Move to CMT or Sentiance at scale. Don’t build telematics.

IPHONE REALITY

A Shortcuts NFC automation turns on Driving Focus. Blocking apps needs Apple’s Screen Time approval.

CTO view. The freshener stays dumb on purpose: an NFC sticker that costs under 35 cents and needs no certification. The phone does the work. We buy driving detection and scoring from an SDK vendor rather than build it. The two pieces we must own are the consent vault, because that’s our legal license to monetize, and the link into our existing lead platform. No third-party app can lock an iPhone, so we never promise that.
